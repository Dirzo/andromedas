ANDRO
MEDAS

Asteroid 2291 Kestrel, out in the Andromedas. Two mining crews, one rock, and a lot of ground in between.
  1. Nothing valuable sits on the surface. Every deposit is buried in the crust under an amber site marker. Your home site is already staked.
  2. Train a surveyor at your command hub. Right-click a site marker to survey it, or switch on auto survey (U) and let it roam. Surveying stakes the site so drones can dig. What lies below is different every match.
  3. Surveyors scan the ground around them constantly and paint a colour map of what lies below: deep blue is barren rock, orange ore, blue titanium, gold platinum, cyan ice, green radium. Strike your first metal to unlock deep scans; deep-scan a site to core-drill it and open its deep zone, where iridium and xenocrystal form.
  4. Right-click a staked site with drones and they dig down into it, terrace by terrace, hauling out metal as they strike it. Ore builds your base; gold only comes from selling cargo to the Space Command freighter.
  5. Each ore opens a branch. Titanium for rovers, platinum for walkers, helium ice for skiffs that fly, radium for long-range mortars, and deep iridium or xenocrystal to forge your mega unit.
  6. Three cards shape your army. Your first metal picks a barracks specialist, your second a tech building that sells research, and your first deep metal unlocks the Deep forge. Specialist and tech building together decide which of nine mega units you can build.
  7. Play as Humans, the Space Fowl or the Cephalopods, all on the same tech tree. Birds are cheaper, faster and frailer; they hatch from eggs, hit up to 10% harder in a flock and regrow feathers out of combat. Cephalopods are pricier, slower and tougher with longer reach; wounded ones squirt ink clouds that shield their squad, and they regrow lost limbs.
  8. The Vessk are void-touched predators: idle hunters cloak, strike from stealth for extra damage, and every kill pays them gold and ore, but their thralls dig slowly. Surveyors, sensor stations, turrets and command buildings reveal cloaked units nearby.
  9. The enemy base lies over the horizon, and the rest of the asteroid is unclaimed ground. Level every enemy structure to win.

FIELD
MANUAL

What you need to know while the rock is still in one piece.
  1. Drones dig open pits. A cell can only go one layer deeper than its neighbours, so pits widen as they deepen. The richest metal sits deepest.
  2. Signals hint at what a buried site holds. Surveying stakes it for digging. A deep scan shows every hidden vein, glowing through the rock.
  3. Click a site to read its core sample. Hatched bands are rock you have not exposed or scanned yet. Metal glints in the pit walls as it is uncovered.
  4. A survey station hears signals from 24 units away and can survey or scan from a distance.
  5. Build ore silos next to far pits so drones spend less time hauling. Nothing can be built on a staked site.
  6. Core drilling (surveyor, C) works on sites you have deep-scanned, and deep scans unlock with your first metal. At a drilled site drones blast waste rock and low-grade ore aside instead of hauling it, so the pit drops fast. The assay tray and hologram show every layer under the drill line. Below layer 5 lies the deep zone: the first deep metal you reach unlocks the Deep forge for your mega unit, and iridium gives it an iridium hull; xenocrystal also unlocks the forge, and gives your mega a xeno core.
  7. Rovers and mortars cannot hit skiffs. Troopers, turrets, walkers and other skiffs can.
  8. Specialists: breachers brawl up close, rangers snipe and hit aircraft, grenadiers splash ground groups. Tech buildings: the Armory sells weapon research, the Engineering bay armour and repair, the Logistics hub speed and income.
  9. Megas: Warlord, Bastion, Rhino (breachers); Longbow, Sentinel, Seraph (rangers); Inferno, Juggernaut, Stormwing (grenadiers), with the Armory, Engineering bay and Logistics hub in that order. Hover the survey chip to see your path.
  10. The freighter arrives on a schedule shown top right. You need a standing command hub to trade.
